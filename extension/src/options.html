Browser control

This extension lets a conversation on your LLM Gateway act in this browser — with your logins. It does nothing until you pair a gateway here and switch it on from the toolbar icon.

Your gateway

Only these origins may drive the extension. Chrome asks for permission per origin; that dialog is the pairing, and it is the one part a web page cannot fake.

    Site access

    A site you grant is a site the assistant may work on — read it, and click, type and submit on it. There is no second dialog per action: a prompt for every click is one nobody reads by the third, and a consent clicked away reflexively protects no one.

    Approved sites

    Approve a site before asking the assistant to work there. Chrome must ask you from this page — a permission cannot be granted while a conversation is waiting.

      What keeps this in check

      The extension does nothing until you switch it on from the toolbar, and that switch is a click no web page can produce. While it is on the icon is green, Chrome shows its own bar over the assistant's window, and every step is listed in the popup and recorded in the gateway's audit trail. Switching it off releases everything at once.

      Worth knowing: a page the assistant reads can contain instructions aimed at it ("open … and click …"). With a site granted, those are carried out without asking. Grant what you would let an assistant work in, and switch it off when you are done.
